# Asian Indian vs. white applicants, Georgia 2023

Georgia 2023: banks denied 10.8% of Asian Indian applicants vs. 5.8% of white applicants (before accounting for finances). Model A's false-alarm ratio, Asian Indian vs. white: 0.49× (95% range 0.28–0.76).

| group        |   applications |   bank denied % |   model A false alarms % (bank-approved, wrongly flagged) |
|:-------------|---------------:|----------------:|----------------------------------------------------------:|
| Asian Indian |            907 |            10.8 |                                                         2 |
| White        |          49609 |             5.8 |                                                         4 |

Asian Indian = HMDA `applicant_race-1` code 21, non-Hispanic. White = the project's White group. A ratio near 1 means model A does not over-flag bank-approved Asian Indian applicants. Small group, so the range is wide.
