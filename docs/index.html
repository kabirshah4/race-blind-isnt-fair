Our fixed AI can't see the neighborhood at all, so here its answer can't change with the address by design. What matters is the result across all 117,547 real 2023 applications: the typical AI wrongly flagged qualified Black applicants 3.0× as often as qualified white applicants; our fixed AI brought that to 1.2× while keeping 97% of the PR-AUC score. The trade-off: qualified white applicants are flagged a little more often (4.0% → 5.6%).
Both AIs run live in your browser: these are the exact trained XGBoost models (every decision tree), exported from Python and checked to give identical answers. Code, data pipeline and write-up: github.com/kabirshah4/race-blind-isnt-fair.
